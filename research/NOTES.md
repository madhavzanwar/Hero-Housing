## 3. Regulation

### 3a. RBI (Housing Finance Companies) Directions, 2025 [OFFICIAL]
URL: https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12939. Reference RBI/DoR/2025-26/365, DoR.FIN.REC.284/03-10-119/2025-26, dated 28-Nov-2025, updated as on 01-Oct-2026.
- Para 3: the Directions apply to HFCs registered under s.29A of the NHB Act, 1987.
- Para 5: the NBFC Directions 2025 also apply to HFCs. These include KYC, Credit Information Reporting, Responsible Business Conduct and Credit Risk Management (the last includes CERSAI). Reporting to "supervisor" means reporting to NHB.
- Para 16: minimum Net Owned Fund is Rs 20 crore. Para 20: CRAR at least 15%, of which Tier 1 at least 10%.
- Para 22 (risk weights, individual housing loans): up to Rs 30 lakh, 35% if LTV ≤80% and 50% if LTV >80–90%. Rs 30–75 lakh with LTV ≤80% (sanctioned on or after 1-Aug-2017): 35%. Above Rs 75 lakh with LTV ≤75% (sanctioned on or after 1-Aug-2017): 50%.
- Para 58: LTV caps of 90%, 80% and 75% for the three loan tiers, with Note (1) on computation and stamp duty and Note (2) pointing to para 215 for valuation.
- Para 129: the Fair Practices Code covers every product and channel, including Digital Lending Platforms.
- Para 131–132: the lender must disclose the "all in cost": processing fees, refundable fees, pre-payment charges, penal charges, fixed↔floating conversion charges and reset clauses.
- Para 133: all particulars should normally be collected at application; if more are needed, the customer must be told immediately.
- Para 135: reasons for rejection must be given in writing.
- Para 136: disbursement must follow the schedule in the sanction letter or loan agreement.
- Para 137: if terms change to the borrower's disadvantage, the borrower may close or switch the loan within 60 days without extra charges.
- Para 142: written complaints must be acknowledged or answered within a week.
- Para 144: a borrower may escalate to the NHB Complaint Redressal Cell after one month.
- Para 163: no pre-payment levy on floating-rate housing loans pre-closed from any source, or on fixed-rate loans pre-closed from the borrower's own sources.
- Para 165: an MITC (Most Important Terms & Conditions) document is required in addition to the KFS.
- Para 197: credit-information reporting follows the NBFC Credit Information Reporting Directions.
- Para 213: CERSAI filing per Chapter VII of the NBFC Credit Risk Management Directions.
- Para 215: Board-approved valuation policy and empanelment of valuers (Annex V).
- Para 217: technical specifications for Account Aggregator participants.
- Paras 234 and 236: NHB supervises and inspects HFCs. Para 235: RBI and NHB can impose penalties under NHB Act s.52A.
- Bank risk-weight circular "Individual Housing Loans – Rationalisation of Risk Weights" (16-Oct-2020), extended for sanctions up to 31-Mar-2023 by the circular of 8-Apr-2022: 35% at LTV ≤80%, 50% at LTV >80–90%. [OFFICIAL index page, not opened] https://website.rbi.org.in/web/rbi/-/notifications/individual-housing-loans-rationalisation-of-risk-weights-12286

### 3b. NBFC Responsible Business Conduct Directions, 2025 (applied to HFCs via HFC para 130/134) [OFFICIAL]
URL: https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12942. Reference RBI/DOR/2025-26/362, 28-Nov-2025, updated as on 1-Jul-2026.
- Para 10: communicate in the vernacular or a language the borrower understands.
- Para 12: acknowledge every loan application, preferably stating the time frame for disposal.
- Para 13: the sanction letter must be in writing, giving the amount, terms, annualised rate of interest and method of application; the borrower's acceptance must be kept on record; late-payment penalties must be in bold in the loan agreement.
- Para 14: give the borrower a copy of the loan agreement and all enclosures.
- Para 29: the KFS rules, carried forward from the 15-Apr-2024 circular.
- Para 31: floating-rate EMI reset for personal loans (home loans included in the example). At sanction, explain how a benchmark change affects EMI and/or tenor. At reset, offer EMI increase, tenor extension, a mix, or prepayment, and a switch to fixed rate as per Board policy. No negative amortisation.
- Paras 33–34: no pre-payment charges on non-business floating-rate loans to individuals.
- Original floating-rate reset circular: RBI/2023-24/55, DOR.MCS.REC.32/01.01.003/2023-24, 18-Aug-2023, to be implemented by 31-Dec-2023. [OFFICIAL] https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12529

### 3c. Key Facts Statement circular [OFFICIAL]
URL: https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12663&Mode=0. Reference RBI/2024-25/18, DOR.STR.REC.13/13.03.00/2024-25, 15-Apr-2024. Addressees include "All Non-Banking Financial Companies (including Housing Finance Companies)."
- Para 2: covers all retail and MSME term loans from all regulated entities.
- Para 4: the KFS follows a standard format (Annex A), in a language the borrower understands; the lender explains it and takes an acknowledgement.
- Para 5: unique proposal number; validity of at least 3 working days for tenors of 7 days or more.
- Para 6: APR computation sheet and amortisation schedule; the APR includes all charges levied by the lender.
- Para 7: third-party charges such as insurance and legal fees are included in the APR and disclosed separately.
- Para 8: no charge outside the KFS without the borrower's explicit consent.
- Para 9: the KFS goes in as a summary box in the loan agreement.
- Para 11: applies to all new loans sanctioned on or after 1-Oct-2024.

### 3d. Digital Lending Directions, 2025 [OFFICIAL]
URL: https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12848. Reference RBI/2025-26/36, DOR.STR.REC.19/21.07.001/2025-26, 8-May-2025.
- Applicability: "All Non-Banking Financial Companies (including Housing Finance Companies)". The definition of digital lending includes automated "credit assessment, loan approval".
- Para 7: assess creditworthiness from the borrower's economic profile, at minimum age, occupation and income, and keep it on record. No automatic increase in credit limit.
- Para 8: the KFS, sanction letter and other documents flow automatically to the borrower's registered email or SMS.
- Para 9: disburse only to the borrower's bank account; no pass-through or pool accounts.
- Para 10: cooling-off period of at least 1 day, set by the Board.
- Para 11: nodal grievance redressal officers; escalation to RB-IOS after 30 days.
- Para 12: data collection must be need-based, with prior explicit consent and an audit trail. No access to contacts, call logs or files. One-time access to camera, microphone or location only for onboarding/KYC. Consent can be revoked and data deleted. No biometric storage unless allowed by law.
- Para 13: the LSP stores only minimal data; data-retention and breach policies are disclosed.
- Para 6 (multi-lender LSPs) applies from 1-Nov-2025; para 17 (DLA directory) from 15-Jun-2025.

### 3e. KYC: NBFC Know Your Customer Directions, 2025 [OFFICIAL]
URL: https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12943. Reference RBI/DOR/2025-26/361, DOR.AML.REC.No.280/14.01.003/2025-26, 28-Nov-2025, updated as on 29-Dec-2025.
- Para 5: definitions. OVDs are passport, driving licence, proof of possession of Aadhaar, Voter ID, NREGA job card and NPR letter. V-CIP is "on par with face-to-face CIP". Digital KYC means a live photo with latitude and longitude.
- Para 18: the Customer Acceptance Policy must not lead to denial of service to disadvantaged people; reasons for rejecting an onboarding must be recorded.
- Para 19: if CDD would tip off the customer, file an STR with FIU-IND instead.
- Para 23: individuals provide Aadhaar or an OVD plus PAN or Form 60. Explanation 1 requires the Aadhaar number to be redacted where authentication is not required.
- Para 27: V-CIP standards. Liveness and spoof detection, face matching, a trained official, varied questions, Aadhaar XML or QR no older than 3 working days, and Aadhaar redaction.
- Para 42: periodic KYC update at least every 2 years for high-risk, 8 years for medium-risk and 10 years for low-risk customers.
- Para 43: PAN or Form 60 is required for existing customers.
- Para 47: keep transaction records and identity records for at least 5 years.
- Para 49: report Rule 3 PML (Maintenance of Records) Rules information to Director, FIU-IND.
- Para 50: file CTR/STR using FIU-IND formats and utilities.
- Para 51: each day of reporting delay is a separate violation.
- Para 53: transaction-monitoring alerts are mandatory.
- Para 54: UAPA s.51A; no accounts for names on the UNSC lists; asset-freeze procedure per the 2-Feb-2021 Order.
- Para 63: upload KYC to CKYCR within 10 days (PML Rule 9(1A)); CERSAI publishes the CKYCR templates and guidelines. The 29-Dec-2025 amendment says the entity that last uploaded or updated the record is responsible for verification; an NBFC that downloads it need not re-verify identity or address.
- UIDAI masked Aadhaar: the first 8 digits are replaced with "xxxx-xxxx" and only the last 4 are visible (e-Aadhaar). [OFFICIAL] https://uidai.gov.in/en/283-english-uk/faqs/aadhaar-online-services/e-aadhaar/1887-what-is-masked-aadhaar.html

### 3f. Data protection: DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025 [OFFICIAL – PIB backgrounder, 17-Nov-2025]
URL: https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf
- The Rules were notified 14-Nov-2025 with an 18-month phased compliance period.
- A separate, plain-language consent notice is required, stating the specific purpose.
- Consent Managers must be India-based companies.
- Affected individuals must be told of a breach without delay, covering what happened, the impact, the steps taken and a contact.
- Data-principal requests (access, correction, erasure) must be answered within 90 days.
- A contact for data queries must be displayed (a designated officer or DPO).
- Maximum penalties: Rs 250 crore for security-safeguard failure, Rs 200 crore for failing to notify a breach, Rs 50 crore for other violations.
- The 72-hour detailed breach report to the Board (Rule 7) comes from secondary sources only; see Unverified.

### 3h. CERSAI [OFFICIAL]
NBFC Credit Risk Management Directions 2025 (RBI/DOR/2025-26/350), Chapter VII, paras 25–28. https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12954
- CERSAI is a s.8 Government company that operates the SARFAESI Central Registry.
- The registry is open to search by any lender, to prevent multiple lending against the same property.
- SARFAESI s.23 requires every security-interest transaction to be filed.
- The 22-Jan-2016 notification extends filing to mortgages other than by deposit of title deeds, and to under-construction property.
- Para 28: file charges on all current transactions on an ongoing basis.
- CERSAI also runs CKYCR operations; see 3e, para 63.
